How the plan adapts

  • Ramp cap: a long run never exceeds 12% above the longest of your last three completed long runs, so a missed run repeats distance until you catch up.
  • Two misses in a row (under 70% of plan) lower the reference by 25%.
  • Fatigue: two or more 8km+ runs in 14 days well above the easy heart-rate ceiling cut the next three long runs by 10%.
  • Volume shortfall: two weeks under 60% of planned distance cut the next three long runs by 15%.
  • Plans are never increased above the original, and race week is never changed. Run config/overrides.json to flag a niggle or set a distance by hand.

Route maps hide the first and last 500 m of each run. General training guidance only, not medical advice. Data from Apple Health.
